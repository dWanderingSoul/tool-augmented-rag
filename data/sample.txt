Company Travel & Conference Guidelines
======================================

Flight Policy:
- Economy class is standard for flights under 8 hours.
- All international flights should be booked at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of travel.

Hotel Policy:
- Maximum hotel budget is $300 USD per night for international travel.
- Hotels within 5km of the conference venue are preferred.

Per Diem Rates:
- East Africa (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda): $80/day for meals
- West Africa (Nigeria, Ghana): $70/day for meals

Conference Logistics:
- Lagos to Nairobi is a common route for company conferences.
- Nairobi Serena Hotel and Radisson Blu are approved company hotels in Nairobi.
- Standard conference stay is 3 days with arrival and departure days counted separately.

Currency Notes:
- All reimbursements are processed in USD.
- 1 USD = approximately 1,400 NGN (Nigerian Naira)
- 1 USD = approximately 130 KES (Kenyan Shilling)
