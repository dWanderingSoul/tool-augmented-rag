Company Travel & Conference Guidelines
======================================

Flight Policy:
- Economy class is standard for flights under 8 hours.
- All international flights should be booked at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Approved Lagos to Nairobi route: Ethiopian Airlines or Kenya Airways.

Hotel Policy:
- Maximum hotel budget is $300 USD per night for international travel.
- Approved hotels in Nairobi: Nairobi Serena Hotel ($250/night), Radisson Blu ($200/night).

Per Diem Rates:
- East Africa (Kenya, Tanzania): $80/day for meals and incidentals.
- West Africa (Nigeria, Ghana): $70/day for meals and incidentals.

Currency:
- All reimbursements processed in USD.
- 1 USD = 1,400 NGN | 1 USD = 130 KES | 1 USD = 0.92 EUR
